The intent gate is rebuilt around one clear warning instead of a puzzle. During the wait you see a single short message; opening is now a slide-to-open that appears only once the countdown ends. The warning reflects how heavily you have used watched apps today, drawn from a wide, randomly varied set of lines. Grayscale now applies before the app is revealed, so the feed no longer flashes in colour. The weekly usage report shows a running total under both per-day and per-app.
