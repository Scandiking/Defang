New: unlock watched apps by scanning an NFC tag instead of the slide. Register a tag under Settings → NFC tag unlock (a cheap NFC sticker, or a card with a fixed ID), place it somewhere out of reach, and opening a watched app becomes a deliberate physical act rather than a thumb flick. Secure cards that change their ID each tap — national ID, passports, most bank cards — are rejected during registration with an explanation. If a tag can't be read because an app blocks the scanner, a slide-to-open backup appears after a few seconds so you are never locked out. NFC stays optional; without it the slide works as before.
